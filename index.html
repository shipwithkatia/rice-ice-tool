RICE / ICE Prioritization

Score, rank, and defend what you build next — everything here runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

How the scoring works
RICE — Reach (people affected per period) × Impact (0.25–3 scale) × Confidence (as a %), divided by Effort (person-months). Higher score, higher priority.
Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort
Pick one period for Reach (e.g. "per quarter") and use it consistently for every feature in this list — mixing periods makes scores impossible to compare.
RICE scores aren't on a fixed 0–100 scale — a "960" means nothing by itself. It only matters compared to the other RICE scores in this same list, which is why each score also shows as a filled bar relative to your highest-scoring feature. With only one feature in the list, there's nothing to compare it to, so no bar is shown.
ICE — a faster, rougher cut: Impact, Confidence, and Ease, each scored 1–10, averaged.
Score = (Impact + Confidence + Ease) ÷ 3
ICE scores are always out of 10, so a "7.0" does mean something on its own — unlike RICE.
